## 1. Design Objective & Experience Principles

### Design Objective
Create a clear, reassuring clinic experience that helps patients find doctors and book appointments effortlessly, while giving doctors and administrators efficient tools for their daily workflows.

### Core Experience Principles
* **Make the next step obvious:** Search $\rightarrow$ Choose a time $\rightarrow$ Confirm.
* **Use plain language:** Present appointment statuses, validation alerts, and error messages clearly.
* **Role-distinct workflows:** Keep Patient, Doctor, and Administrator views tailored to their specific responsibilities (each role has its own sidebar menu and start page).
* **Progressive disclosure & privacy:** Show only information needed for the current task and protect sensitive medical details.
* **Responsive by default:** Every screen adapts from desktop to 375 px phones; the sidebar and landing navigation collapse into a menu.
* **Dark / Light mode:** follows the device setting by default; a toggle in the sidebar saves the user's choice.

## 4. UI/UX & Accessibility Guidelines (`WCAG 2.2 AA`)

* **Navigation & Layout:** sidebar workspace per role; generous spacing; related items grouped in cards; navigation collapses into an accessible menu on mobile.
* **Forms & Feedback:** persistent `<label>` above every input; server-side validation messages in plain language under the field; success / error alerts at the top of the page; statuses shown with text, not color alone.
* **Accessibility Compliance:** WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (navy `#14248A` and text `#0F172A` on white; white on navy buttons); 44 px minimum targets; full keyboard navigation with visible focus rings; ARIA labels on icon-only buttons; `aria-live` region for live notifications; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.

### Prototype Validation Checklist
| Task | Validation Check |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Find a doctor** | Can a patient enter a specialty/location (or a free day) and immediately understand the filtered results? |
| **Book an appointment** | Can a patient choose an open time slot, review details, and recognize booking confirmation? |
| **Manage schedule** | Can a doctor identify upcoming appointments and manage weekly availability and days off? |
| **Review activity** | Can an administrator find appointment totals, charts, and export a report? |
| **Accessibility** | Can users navigate entirely by keyboard and identify labels, focus states, errors, and statuses? |
