## 1. Getting started

### 1.1 The home page
Open the site in any modern browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox, Safari). The home page explains the service and has
**Log In** and **Get Started** buttons. When you are signed in, it shows your name, your role and **Go to Dashboard**.

![Home page](../docs/screenshots/01-landing.png)

### 1.2 Create an account
1. Click **Get Started**.
2. Choose **Patient** or **Doctor** and click **Continue**.
   ![Select role](../docs/screenshots/02-select-role.png)
3. Enter your full name, email and a password (at least 6 characters), confirm the password, and click **Sign up**.
   ![Sign up](../docs/screenshots/03-signup.png)
4. You are taken to the login page with a confirmation message.

> **Doctors:** after signing up, complete your profile (specialty, clinic address, fee). An administrator must approve
> your account before patients can find and book you.
>
> **Administrators** are created by the clinic, not from the sign-up page.

### 1.3 Log in
Enter your email and password and click **Log in**, or use **Continue with Google** if the clinic enabled it.
After logging in you go straight to your own area: patients to the dashboard, doctors to their appointments,
administrators to the analytics dashboard.

![Log in](../docs/screenshots/04-login.png)

### 1.4 Forgot your password?
1. On the login page click **Forget Password?**
2. Enter your email and click **Send code**.
   ![Forgot password](../docs/screenshots/05-forgot-password.png)
3. Check your email for a **4-digit code**. Type it on the **Verify Account** page.
   The code expires after **10 minutes** and allows **5 tries**; use **Resend code** to get a new one.
4. Choose a new password and confirm it. You can now log in with the new password.

### 1.5 Pages you can't open
Each role only sees its own pages. If you open a page that belongs to another role (for example a patient opening a
doctor page) you see **Access denied**, with a button back to your own area or to sign in with another account.

![Access denied](../docs/screenshots/19-access-denied.png)

## 2. Patient guide

### 2.1 Dashboard
After logging in you see your name, a search box for doctors, quick tiles (**Doctor**, **Medical History**) and your
**next appointment** (doctor, specialty, time and clinic). The sidebar opens every patient page; the orange number next to
**Notifications** is how many you haven't read.

![Patient dashboard](../docs/screenshots/10-patient-dashboard.png)

### 2.2 Find a doctor
Open **Doctor** (or **See all**). Filter by:
- **Specialty** (the clinic's list),
- **Doctor name**,
- **Location**,
- **Free on**: a date. Only doctors who still have a free time that day are shown.

Each card shows the doctor's specialty, clinic and consultation fee. Click **Book**.

![Find a doctor](../docs/screenshots/11-find-doctor.png)

### 2.3 Book an appointment
1. Pick a **date**. The free 30-minute times for that day load immediately.
2. Choose a **time** and (optionally) write the **reason for your visit**.
3. Click **Book appointment**.

The card on the right shows the doctor, specialty, clinic and fee. Your request is **Pending** until the doctor confirms
it; you get a notification (and an email) when they do. The same time can never be booked twice.

![Book an appointment](../docs/screenshots/12-book-appointment.png)

### 2.4 My appointments: cancel or reschedule
**Appointments** lists upcoming and past visits with their status
(Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Rejected, Cancelled, No-show).
- **Reschedule**: pick a new day and time with the same doctor (the appointment goes back to *Pending*).
- **Cancel**: asks for confirmation; the doctor is notified.

![My appointments](../docs/screenshots/13-my-appointments.png)

### 2.5 Prescriptions and medical records
- **Medical History / Medical Records** shows the diagnosis and notes the doctor wrote for each completed visit.
- **My Prescriptions** lists medications with dosage, frequency and number of days. Click **Print** for a clean,
  printer-friendly copy.

![Medical records](../docs/screenshots/15-medical-records.png)
![Prescriptions](../docs/screenshots/14-my-prescriptions.png)

### 2.6 My documents (test results, X-rays, reports)
1. Open **Documents**.
2. Enter a **title**, choose the **type** (Test result, X-ray, Report, Other) and pick the file.
3. Click **Upload**.

Allowed files: **PDF, JPG, PNG**, up to **10 MB**. Use **View** to open a file and **Download** to save it.
Only you and the doctors you booked with can see your files. Doctors can also add files to your record.

![Documents](../docs/screenshots/16-documents.png)

### 2.7 My profile and notification settings
**My Profile** holds your personal and medical information: date of birth, gender, mobile number, blood type,
**allergies**, chronic conditions, current medications, medical history and an emergency contact. Your doctors see it
when you book with them, and allergies are shown to them first.

At the bottom, choose whether you also want **emails** and **text messages (SMS)**. In-app notifications are always on.
Add your mobile number with the country code (for example `+201001234567`) to receive SMS reminders.

![My profile](../docs/screenshots/17-patient-profile.png)

## 3. Doctor guide

### 3.1 Complete your profile (new doctors)
Open **My Profile**, choose your **specialty** from the list, and enter your **clinic address** and **consultation fee**.
Click **Save profile**. A notice shows that you are *waiting for approval* until an administrator approves you.

![Doctor profile](../docs/screenshots/26-doctor-profile.png)

### 3.2 Working hours and days off
Open **Working Hours**.
- **Add** a time range for each weekday you work (for example Sunday 09:00–13:00). Patients can book 30-minute slots
  inside these ranges.
- **Days off**: add a date (and an optional reason) for vacations or conferences. No times are offered on that day.
  If patients are already booked that day, the system asks you to handle those appointments first.

![Working hours and days off](../docs/screenshots/22-working-hours.png)

### 3.3 Your appointments
**Appointments** shows today's count, pending requests and confirmed upcoming visits.
- **Confirm** or **Reject** new requests (the patient is notified).
- After the visit, mark it **Completed**, or **No-show** if the patient didn't come.
- Switch to **Calendar** to see your week, day or month. Colors show the status.

![Doctor appointments](../docs/screenshots/20-doctor-appointments.png)
![Doctor calendar](../docs/screenshots/21-doctor-calendar.png)

### 3.4 Patient details
Click a **patient's name** to open their details: **allergies** (in red), chronic conditions, medications, history,
contact information, every visit with you, and their documents. You can **add a file** (for example a lab report) to the
patient's record. You only see patients who booked with you.

![Patient details](../docs/screenshots/23-patient-details.png)

### 3.5 Medical record and prescription
For a **Completed** visit:
- **Medical record**: write the **diagnosis** and **notes**, then **Save record**. The patient sees it under Medical Records.
- **Prescription**: add each medication (name, dosage, frequency, days). The patient sees it under My Prescriptions.

![Medical record](../docs/screenshots/24-medical-record.png)
![Prescription](../docs/screenshots/25-prescription.png)

## 4. Administrator guide

### 4.1 Dashboard and reports
The **Dashboard** shows totals (patients, approved doctors, appointments, completed visits, revenue) and charts:
- appointments per month (all, completed, cancelled/rejected)
- revenue from completed visits
- most popular doctors and specialties
- appointments by status
- patients by gender and age
- new patients per month

Choose a **From / To** range and click **Update**. **PDF** and **Excel** download the same report.

![Admin dashboard](../docs/screenshots/30-admin-dashboard.png)

### 4.2 Doctors
**Doctors** lists every doctor, pending ones first. **Approve** a doctor once their profile is complete (specialty and
clinic address). **Remove approval** hides a doctor from patients.

![Admin doctors](../docs/screenshots/31-admin-doctors.png)

### 4.3 Patients
**Patients**: search by name or email. **Block** stops a patient from signing in (their data stays);
**Unblock** restores access.

![Admin patients](../docs/screenshots/32-admin-patients.png)

### 4.4 All appointments
Filter by **status**, **dates** and **patient or doctor name**. **Cancel** a pending or confirmed appointment;
the patient and the doctor are both notified.

![Admin appointments](../docs/screenshots/33-admin-appointments.png)

### 4.5 Specializations
Add a specialization (name and optional description), rename one (its doctors move with it), or untick **Shown** to hide
it from the doctor and search lists.

![Admin specializations](../docs/screenshots/34-admin-specializations.png)

## 5. Notifications and reminders

| When | Who is notified | How |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A patient books, reschedules or cancels | Doctor | In-app, live |
| The doctor confirms or rejects | Patient | In-app, live, email |
| The visit is completed | Patient | In-app, live |
| 24 hours before a confirmed appointment | Patient | In-app, email, SMS |
| The clinic cancels an appointment | Patient and doctor | In-app, live (email/SMS to the patient) |

- **Live:** a pop-up appears immediately on any open page, and the counter in the sidebar updates without reloading.
- Open **Notifications** to see them all. Click one to open its page, or **Mark all as read**.
- Email and SMS follow the patient's choices in **My Profile**.

![Notifications](../docs/screenshots/18-notifications.png)

## 7. Troubleshooting and FAQ

| Problem | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Invalid email or password." | Check the email and password. After several wrong tries the account locks for a while; use **Forget Password?** |
| I didn't receive the reset code | Check spam, wait a minute and click **Resend code**. The code expires after 10 minutes. |
| I can't find a doctor | Clear the filters. New doctors appear only after an administrator approves them. |
| No times on the day I want | The doctor doesn't work that day, is on a day off, or is fully booked. Try another date or use **Free on** in the search. |
| My upload was refused | Only real PDF, JPG or PNG files up to 10 MB are accepted. |
| I see "Access denied" | The page belongs to another role. Use the button to go to your own area. |
| I don't get emails or SMS | Check **My Profile → Notifications** and your mobile number (with country code). In-app notifications always work. |
| The right password still doesn't work | Your account may be blocked by the clinic (the message is the same for security). Contact the clinic administrator. |
